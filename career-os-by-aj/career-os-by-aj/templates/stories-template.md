# STAR Story Bank

Pre-built stories for interview preparation. Each story follows the STAR format and is tagged for quick retrieval.

## How to Use
- Stories are added here over time as you prepare for interviews and debrief after them
- Use `/debrief` after interviews to capture new stories
- Each story includes tags so Career OS can suggest relevant ones for specific interview questions
